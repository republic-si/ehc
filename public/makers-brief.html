The new packaging rules · 4 of 8

New, and it lands on 12 August 2026

Every country you sell into now wants its own packaging paperwork. Sell through us and you file none of it.

No exemption

The EU Packaging Regulation (2025/40) applies from 12 August 2026. Micro-producers are excused some of it, but not the producer responsibility fees.

Country by country

You register, report and pay in every member state your bottles reach. Where you are not established, you must appoint an authorised representative there.

And the penalties

Germany alone fines up to €200,000 for failing to register or join a dual system, and every member state sets its own.

The fees themselves are small. German registration is free and a small maker’s licensing can be under €100 a year. The cost is the multiplication: one maker, six countries, six registers, six reports, six sets of rates. And from 2030 an e-commerce parcel may be no more than 50% empty space — a glass-bottle problem worth designing for now, not retrofitting later.

On every order that goes through us, this stops being your problem

The obligation falls on whoever first puts the packaging on the market in a country. When your bottle sells through us, we are the ones placing it there — so we register, we report, we pay. You do not appear in any of those six registers. Not for one country, not for six.

That covers sales we make. For the sales you make yourself, we are negotiating a single group rate with a licensed compliance provider so that members get one contract, one price and one phone number instead of six of each. That deal is not signed yet, and we will show you the numbers when it is.

And the longer route

We are already working on the shared route through it.

As Republic of Heat’s B2C volume grows, it gives us the scale to pay for registrations, reporting and compliant packaging centrally — then spread that work and cost across the network. We do not have every country solved yet. The point is that each maker should not have to solve the same problem alone.

What already works · 5 of 8

So how, exactly, are you supposed to grow?

You make the sauce. Then you buy the boxes, book the courier, eat the postage, learn the customs codes, register for packaging in six separate countries, chase the invoice, answer the emails, work the stall, shoot the photos, and start again on Monday. On an €8.84 bottle.

It is ridiculous. It is why hot sauce in Europe stays a hobby for most of us.

EHC exists to break that cycle. Not to make the grind easier — to build a business that can grow beyond your own two hands.
